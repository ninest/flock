Enrichment

Open Pricing with a token, an email and UTM tags in the URL. Flock stores the Pageview's URL with only the utm_* parameters left, and adds $country, $browser, $os and $device from the request (never the IP itself). See them under Audience on the dashboard. Visiting from localhost or a private network gives an unknown country.
